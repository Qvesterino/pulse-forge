# First fifteen minutes

From an empty browser tab to a playing, exported beat.

## 0. Open the studio

Open the KYX URL. The **project browser** is the first screen: pick a template
on the left (House, Techno, Trap, Ambient, UKG, Jersey, Phonk, Drill, Lo-fi
House, Reggaeton, Boom Bap, DnB, Scene Score, or Empty) and click it. Each
template ships a working starting point — drums, instruments, a mixer, scenes.

First visit only: a landing page introduces the studio. Click through; you can
skip it forever afterwards.

## 1. Press play

`Space` plays and pauses. The transport bar at the top shows position, tempo
and the loop bracket. If you hear nothing, check the browser didn't mute the
tab, and click once anywhere in the page (browsers unlock audio on the first
gesture).

## 2. Make it yours — three quick edits

1. **Drums**: click steps in the sequencer grid (bottom) to add and remove
   hits. Drag vertically on a step to change its velocity; `Alt`+drag sets
   micro-timing; `Ctrl`+drag sets step probability.
2. **Melody**: select an instrument track (number keys `1`–`9` switch tracks)
   and click in the piano roll to insert notes. Drag notes to move them, drag
   their right edge to resize.
3. **Arrange**: open the arrangement panel (`Alt+3`) and drag **scene clips**
   on the timeline — each clip plays its scene's pattern for as many bars as
   it is long. This is how a 4-bar loop becomes a song.

## 3. Let the AI help (optional)

Open the **INTENT** panel (`Alt+7`), type what you want in plain language —
"dark techno drive, 128 bpm, rumbling bass" — and generate. Every result is
auditionable and lands in your project as normal, editable content. The **DICE**
panel (`Alt+6`) rolls seeded variations of what you already have: same idea,
fresh take, full undo.

## 4. Mix a little

`Alt+1` opens the mixer: faders, pans, mutes and the effect rack per track.
Pick a track, add an effect from the rack, turn its knobs. The **MASTER**
panel (`Alt+9`) holds the mastering chain — for a first pass, just watch the
meters and keep the master out of the red.

## 5. Save and export

Projects autosave to your browser. Press `S` to force a save (the status bar
confirms). To take music out:

- `Alt+5` opens **Export**: render the song to WAV or MP3, or render selected
  tracks as stems.
- The **Share** option produces a compact code anyone can open in their own
  KYX — your whole project travels as text.
