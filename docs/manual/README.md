# KYX User Manual

Welcome to KYX — a browser-first music production studio. KYX runs entirely in
your browser: compose with the step sequencer and piano roll, arrange scenes and
audio clips, mix with a full effect rack and mastering chain, collaborate live
with other musicians, and export finished tracks.

Nothing to install. Your projects live in your browser's storage on your machine.

## Requirements

- A modern Chromium browser (Chrome or Edge recommended; Firefox works). Safari
  is supported best-effort.
- Audio needs a user gesture: the first click on Play unlocks sound (browser
  policy). Headphones recommended.
- Everything runs locally in your browser — no account, no upload. Live
  collaboration and the public gallery are opt-in.

## Getting help

- Press `?` anywhere for the built-in shortcut help.
- The status bar at the bottom announces what the app is doing (saves, lockups,
  capture offers).
- Projects autosave to your browser's storage; use `S` to force a save before
  closing.
