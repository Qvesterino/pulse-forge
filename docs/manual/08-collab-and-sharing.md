# Collaboration and sharing

## Live jam sessions

Open the COLLAB panel, start a room, and share the room link: everyone who
opens it plays in the same project in real time — transport, arrangement,
patterns, mixer. Changes sync live and the whole session history is undoable
locally.

**Jam roles** keep a session musical: after joining, each player picks a role —
OWNER (everything), DRUMS (pads, steps, patterns), KEYS (notes, instruments),
MIXER (levels, FX, automation), ARRANGE (scenes, clips, markers, tempo). A role
is a focus guard, not a punishment: your UI simply stops your hands from
editing outside your lane. The owner is unrestricted.

## Gallery

Publish a finished track (or a beat with stems) to the built-in gallery: others
can play it, open it in their studio, and remix it. Published items carry
lineage — remixes remember what they came from, and the gallery can show a
track's family tree.

## Battles

Gallery tracks can battle: listeners vote head-to-head, blind. Elo-style
ratings order the feed (sort by battles) — a lightweight way to test material
and find collaborators.

## Etiquette

Live rooms share one undo history per player branch — announce big structural
edits (deleting scenes, changing tempo) in chat before doing them. Roles exist
exactly so that this conversation is rarely needed.
