check the measurement contract. It now reads it from KYX_AUDIT_SOURCE when
that is set, so the guard can be pointed at a DOCTORED COPY and shown to
fail. A guard that cannot be shown to fail is not a guard.

      hostProcesses                                     -> guard fails
